Post a note

Three steps on chain: checkpoint the venue, wait an hour, post. The hour is the point: your baseline is the venue's price history, not the price at the moment you press the button. A note costs the 0.1 IMD stamp, a fixed 0.25 IMD buy of MARG, and your bond, 0.25 to 10 IMD, which comes back if the call lands.

Pools
